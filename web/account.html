Delete your QSpot Marketplace account

You can ask QSpot Technologies Inc. to delete your QSpot Marketplace account and the data linked to it in any of these ways. You don't need to reinstall the app.

  1. In the app: Profile › Settings › Delete Account.
  2. On this page: sign in with the same Google or Apple account you use in the app, then choose Delete my account.
  3. By email: write to admin@qspotmarketplace.com from the email address on your account.

What happens

Your account is scheduled for deletion and is permanently deleted after 7 days. Signing in to the app during those 7 days cancels it. Your account and all associated data (listings, messages, and reviews) are deleted, and any remaining Premium is forfeited. If a purchase, payout or dispute is still in progress, deletion waits until it is settled.

Records of completed payments are kept in a de-identified form for 7 years (10 in the EU) because tax law requires it. They contain no name, email or location.
