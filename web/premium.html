How to join

  1. Choose a plan above.
  2. Sign in below with the Google or Apple account you use in the QSpot app.
  3. Review your plan and pay securely with Stripe.

Secure payment by Stripe. Cancel anytime. Benefits continue to the end of the paid period. The quarterly and yearly plans run for their first 3 months or year, then continue month to month at the monthly rate unless you choose, in a term's last 90 days, to renew it for one more term (Terms, section 11).
