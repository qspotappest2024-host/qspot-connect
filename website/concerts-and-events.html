Concert, festival and game-day lines: trade your spot

General admission doors, merch tables, box offices and stadium gates. If you got there early, your place in line is worth something to someone who couldn't.

Explore the Live Map See how it works

You're already in line

  1. Drop a pin on the line, set your price and add a photo of where you are.
  2. Keep your place and check in once an hour while you wait for a buyer.
  3. Step out when your buyer steps in, and get paid for the time you put in.

You'd rather not wait

  1. Find spots at your venue on the live map.
  2. Check the seller's ratings before you send a request.
  3. Meet at the line, swap places, and you're in.

Why it works on event day

Event lines are long, predictable and full of people who planned ahead. Fans who arrive hours early for general admission or merch can turn that time into money, and fans coming straight from work can still get a good place without camping out.

Because every trade is one-for-one, nobody behind you loses ground. One person steps out, one steps in, and the line stays exactly the same length.

Good to know

  • Payment waits for the handoff. With secure payment, the buyer's card is held and only charged once you're both at the line and the swap is confirmed by GPS.
  • Sellers prove they're still there. A listed spot needs a check-in at least once an hour, and every listing ends after 36 hours at most.
  • Know who you're meeting. Ratings and reviews are on every profile, and an in-app chat opens once a request is accepted.
  • Anywhere in Canada. QSpot Marketplace works for any line in the country, as long as the spot and both people are in Canada.

Questions

How do I sell my spot in line?

Tap Sell in the app's bottom bar. Add photos, set your location and price, and choose a payment model: secure payment (free to list, the buyer pays in the app) or offline payment (a one-time listing fee, and you arrange payment with the buyer yourself).

How do I buy someone's place in line?

Find a spot on the live map, open it, and tap Request to Purchase. Nothing is charged when you send the request. Once the seller accepts, a map and chat help you find each other, and you pay during the transaction.

Does the line get longer?

No. Every trade is one-for-one: the seller steps out the moment the buyer steps in, so the line stays the same length and nobody behind moves back.
