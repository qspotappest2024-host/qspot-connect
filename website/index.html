Payments & safety

Every trade, protected

Money only moves when the swap is real.

Your money waits for the handoff

Book a QSpot Secure spot and Stripe places a temporary hold (a pre-authorization) on your card. You're only charged once the handoff is confirmed on location. If the trade is cancelled, the hold is released.

Ratings and reviews are up front, and sellers earn a verified badge by completing a paid listing or payout.

Your card stays private

Protected payouts

Sellers are paid through Stripe, straight to their own account, when the trade completes. Both buyers and sellers get a short window to report a problem.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How buying, selling and getting paid work on QSpot Marketplace.

Where is QSpot Marketplace available?

QSpot Marketplace is currently available in Canada only, and both the spot and the people involved need to be in Canada. If you're travelling outside the country, listing and buying are unavailable until you're back.

How do I sell my spot in line?

Tap Sell in the app's bottom bar. Add photos, set your location and price, and choose a payment model. Your listing goes live once the form is complete and, for offline payment listings, once the listing fee is paid.

Which payment model should I choose?

Secure payment: listing is free, the buyer pays through the app and can pre-authorize their card so the funds are held until you both complete, and a QSpot Secure Listing Fee is deducted from your payout on each sale. Offline payment: you pay a one-time QSpot Listing Fee upfront (shown before you pay) and arrange payment with the buyer yourself. Choose secure payment if you'd rather not handle money in person.

Do I have to stay in line while my spot is listed?

Yes. You check in at least once an hour while your spot is listed, to confirm you're still holding the place in line. QSpot warns you before the hour is up, and if there's no check-in the listing comes down automatically. Every listing also has a maximum life of 36 hours. You need to be at the spot to check in.

How do I buy someone's place in line?

Find a spot on the live map or in search, open it, and tap Request to Purchase. Your request goes to the seller and nothing is charged yet. Once they accept, the transaction opens with a map and chat so you can find each other. You pay during that transaction, not when you send the request.

When and how do I pay?

It depends on the seller's payment model. Offline payment listings are arranged directly between the two of you, and QSpot doesn't process the money. On secure payment listings you pay in the app: you either pre-authorize your card or pay straight away. A pre-authorization places a temporary hold on your card. The money is only taken once you both complete the transaction, and the hold is released if it's cancelled. Your total includes the QSpot Processing Fee and is shown in full before you confirm. Card payments are handled by Stripe, and QSpot never sees your card details.

What happens when I meet the seller?

You both need to be at the meeting point before the transaction can be completed. The app checks your positions and unlocks Complete Transaction once you're together. You both confirm, any card hold is captured at that moment, and then you rate each other.

Can I cancel?

Yes. A purchase request that hasn't been accepted can be withdrawn at any time, and the seller can decline it. Nothing is charged at that stage. Once a transaction is live, either side can cancel it. If you'd already pre-authorized your card, the hold is released automatically and no money is taken.

How do sellers get paid?

Sellers set up payouts with Stripe from their Profile. On a secure payment sale, the buyer is charged when you both confirm the handoff, and your share (your price minus the QSpot Secure Listing Fee) goes to your Stripe account. Stripe then sends it to your bank on its own payout schedule, which usually includes a holding period of several days plus your bank's processing time.

What if something goes wrong?

If a secure payment transaction didn't go as agreed, open it from History and tap Dispute while the window is still open. The receipt shows a countdown. Add a reason and photos if you have them. The sale is put on hold while our team reviews it.

How do ratings work?

You rate the person, not the spot. After a transaction completes or is cancelled, both sides can leave an optional star rating. Your average rating and review count appear on your profile.

What is QSpot Premium?

QSpot Premium reduces the QSpot Secure Listing Fee on every sale, includes one free offline payment listing each month, and shows member status on your profile. See the plans.
