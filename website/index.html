How it works

One steps out. One steps in. Nobody loses their place.

It's a one-for-one swap, not a cut, so the line never gets longer.

  1. List your spot

    Drop a pin on the line you're in and set your price. Post photos and a description to create demand.

  2. A buyer books it

    Someone nearby claims your exact place. On Secure spots their payment is held.

  3. Swap & get paid

    They step in as you step out. The handoff is confirmed and your payout releases.

  1. Find a spot nearby

    Browse live spots on the map and check the seller's ratings before you commit.

  2. Book it

    Secure spots place a hold on your card. You're only charged at the handoff.

  3. Step in

    Meet the seller at the line, swap places, and you're on your way.

Payments & safety

Every trade, protected

Money only moves when the swap is real.

Your money waits for the handoff

Book a QSpot Secure spot and Stripe places a temporary hold (a pre-authorization) on your card. You're only charged once the handoff is confirmed on location. If the trade is cancelled, the hold is released.

  1. Hold placedwhen you book
  2. Handoffconfirmed by GPS
  3. Paid outto the seller

Know who you're meeting

Ratings and reviews are up front, and sellers earn a verified badge by completing a paid listing or payout.

Your card stays private

Payments run on Stripe, a PCI-certified provider. QSpot Marketplace never sees or stores your card number.

Protected payouts

Sellers are paid through Stripe, straight to their own account, when the trade completes. Both buyers and sellers get a short window to report a problem.

Rather settle in person?

Post a self-managed spot for a small listing fee and arrange payment with your buyer directly.
